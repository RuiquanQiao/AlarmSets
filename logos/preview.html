AlarmSets — concepts

Four directions, not four variations. The strip under each card is the honest test: 48px is the launcher list, 32px is a favicon, and the round crop is the mask Android applies on most phones.

concept-1 — two overlapping bells
The composition you asked for. Says “more than one” instantly. The gap is cut into the back bell’s own outline, so it survives a flat monochrome tint.

Reads as a stack first, a bell second. Widest gaps of the four, so it holds together smallest — but the bell reading is the weakest.

concept-3 — clock face inside a bell
One bold shape, ticks cut as real holes. Most “clock app” of the four, which is also its risk: it looks like every other clock app.

concept-4 — a switch whose knob is a bell
